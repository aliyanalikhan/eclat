## Milestone Goal

Ensure every layout, component, and interactive page reflows seamlessly across all device screen sizes (phone, tablet, desktop, ultra-wide) and preserve & adapt the signature scroll-driven fan carousel animation in `useScrollFx` to scale dynamically without clipping or mobile suppression.

---

## Phases Shipped

### Phase 7: Breakpoint Architecture & Fluid Foundation
- **Goal:** Establish a unified 4-tier responsive breakpoint architecture, fluid clamp() typography and spacing tokens, and guarantee zero horizontal overflow site-wide across all routes.
- **Requirements:** `RESP-01`, `RESP-02`, `RESP-03`
- **Plans:** `07-01-PLAN.md`, `07-02-PLAN.md`
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - `src/lib/breakpoints.js` exporting canonical `BREAKPOINTS`, `QUERIES`, and `useBreakpoint()` hook.
  - Documented 4-tier breakpoint hierarchy in `src/styles/eclat.css`.
  - Fluid typography tokens (`--fs-h1`..`--fs-body`, `--shell: min(100%, 1520px)`, `--shell-pad`).
  - Site-wide `overflow-x: clip;` zero-horizontal-overflow containment on root containers.

### Phase 8: Hero Animation Multi-Device Adaptation
- **Goal:** Preserve the current signature scroll-driven hero fan animation mechanics while adapting geometry, bounding, and card scaling dynamically so it looks elegant and unclipped on phone, tablet, desktop, and ultrawide screens.
- **Requirements:** `HERO-01`, `HERO-02`, `HERO-03`, `HERO-04`
- **Plans:** `08-01-PLAN.md`, `08-02-PLAN.md`
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Removed mobile CSS suppression (`display: none` and `transform: none !important`) and `!mobile` short-circuit in `useScrollFx`.
  - Multi-device responsive fan geometry solver in `src/lib/motion.js` (`fit <= 0.55`, `rotFactor = 0.65`, `bound = vw / 2 - 28px` on phones).
  - Adaptive scroll travel distance (`vh * 0.48` on phones, `vh * 0.54` on tablets, `vh * 0.62` on desktops).
  - Initial mount luxury reveals (`@keyframes heroFadeUp` and `@keyframes fanEntrance`).
  - Strict `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` compliance with single settled pass and immediate side titles opacity.

### Phase 9: Content Pages & Responsive Multi-Device Grids
- **Goal:** Reflow all content listing grids (Editions, Books, Writings, Archive) and editorial reading pages seamlessly across phone, tablet, desktop, and large displays.
- **Requirements:** `GRID-01`, `GRID-02`, `GRID-03`
- **Plans:** `09-01-PLAN.md`, `09-02-PLAN.md`
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Content-aware grid reflow in `src/styles/eclat.css` (2-col compact thumbnail grid for Archive on mobile; 1-col stacked for Books and Writings; 2-col on tablet; 3-col on desktop; 4-col on ultrawide).
  - Horizontal media strips (`.strip`) enhanced with `touch-action: pan-x`, `-webkit-overflow-scrolling: touch`, desktop grab cursor, and luxury edge fade masks (`.strip-wrap`).
  - Harmonized `.ed-hero__grid` to 1023px tablet boundary with single-column centered layout (cover max 280px).
  - Standardized `.prose` reading measure to `max-width: 68ch; line-height: 1.75;`.

### Phase 10: Interactive Overlays & Global Chrome Refinement
- **Goal:** Ensure modals (Magazine Preview flipbook, Lightbox zoom) and global chrome (header nav and footer) adapt flawlessly on any viewport size.
- **Requirements:** `OVERLAY-01`, `OVERLAY-02`, `OVERLAY-03`
- **Plans:** `10-01-PLAN.md`, `10-02-PLAN.md`
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Magazine preview modal (`MagazinePreview.jsx`): Direct touch horizontal swipe gestures, directional slide keyframes (`flipSlideInRight` / `flipSlideInLeft`), adjacent image background preloading, and floating glassmorphism pill controls.
  - Lightbox & Video modals: Hardened iOS scroll lock (`overscroll-behavior: contain; touch-action: none;`), notch safe-area close buttons, and downward swipe-to-dismiss gesture on Lightbox.
  - Global Header: Mobile navigation drawer with slide-down animation, dimmed blurred backdrop overlay (`.nav-backdrop`), backdrop tap-to-close, and body scroll lock.
  - Global Footer: 2-column balanced link grid reflow on mobile (`<768px`) with 44px tap targets and `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` home-indicator padding.
