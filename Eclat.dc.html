The orchard road is closed for the season, which is how you know the season has turned.

For eleven years the bakery on the square opened at five. When the roof went, the village decided that rebuilding it slowly was not a compromise but the point — and so the ovens came back one at a time, over fourteen months, each one lit by whoever had paid for it.

There is a photograph on the wall behind the counter of everyone who carried a brick. It is not a good photograph. Nobody is looking at the camera and the light is wrong and it is, by a distance, the most valuable object in the building.
